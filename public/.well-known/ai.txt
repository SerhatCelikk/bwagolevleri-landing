## Project Summary

BWA Göl Evleri is a 64-unit residential project located on the shores of
Küçükçekmece Lake in Avcılar, Istanbul, Turkey. The project sits on the
Kanal Istanbul route and is developed by BWA GYO — a joint venture of
Barsan (Construction), Winn4 (Investment) and Adproje (Architecture).
